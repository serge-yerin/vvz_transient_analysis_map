# Acknowledgements

The transient catalogue served by this workflow was produced by the **UTR-2
Pulsar/Transient Survey of the Northern Sky (UTPSNS)**, carried out with the
**UTR-2** radio telescope (Ukrainian T-shaped Radio telescope, second
modification) near Hrakove, Kharkiv region, Ukraine, operated by the Institute
of Radio Astronomy of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine.

The 20 MHz galactic background image used as the sky map backdrop originates
from the same survey material.

The analysis reproduces, in Python, the original IDL program
`transientanalisys_380_flux_v2.pro` written by the survey team.

The catalogue and analysis are described in the UTPSNS publication,
DOI [10.1051/0004-6361/202555029](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202555029),
which is set as the `oda:reference` of the MMODA service.
